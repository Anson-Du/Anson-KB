# Hong Kong Top 5 Universities: A-Level Undergraduate Admission Requirements
## Research Summary for 2026 Entry (Compiled May 2025)

> **Important Notes:**
> - A-level holders apply via the **Non-JUPAS** (non-local) route at all UGC-funded universities.
> - **JUPAS** is exclusively for holders of HKDSE (Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education). A-level students cannot use JUPAS regardless of HK residency.
> - HK resident status does NOT change A-level admission pathway — all A-level applicants go through Non-JUPAS.
> - However, HK residents may qualify for **local tuition fees** (subsidized by government) if they meet residency requirements (typically 7+ years of ordinary residence in HK).
> - All figures below are for **2025/26 or 2026/27 entry** and subject to change. Always verify on official websites.

### Required A-Level Subjects
- **Science Faculty** (Mathematics, Chemistry majors): Strong performance in relevant subjects. Mathematics and/or Science subjects expected at A/A* level.

- Excluded subjects: General Studies, Critical Thinking, and similar broad-subject qualifications are NOT counted.

### IELTS / English Language Requirement
- **Minimum IELTS: 6.5** overall (academic module)
- Law and top Business programs effectively require **7.0+**
- Scores must be obtained after **September 1, 2024** for 2026 entry
- A-Level English Language/Literature at grade E or above may satisfy the requirement

- Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Data Analytics programs: One A-Level subject from **Physics or Mathematics**

**Engineering Faculty:**
- Most programs: A-Level **Mathematics** + one from Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or Computer Science

**Business Faculty:**
- Most programs: A-Level **Mathematics** required

**General Requirements:**
- Minimum 3 A-Level subjects (excluding General Studies and Chinese)
- English requirement: A-Level English / English Literature at Grade E (AS or A-Level)

### IELTS / English Language Requirement
- **IELTS: 6.5** overall (Academic module), with no sub-score below **6.0**
- Alternative: A-Level English at Grade E or above

## HK RESIDENT STATUS: KEY ADVANTAGES

| Aspect | HK Resident | Non-Resident |
|---|---|---|
| **Tuition fees** | ~HK$42,100–47,000/year (subsidized) | ~HK$145,000–215,000/year (non-subsidized) |
| **Financial aid** | Eligible for government loans and grants | Limited to university scholarships |
| **Admission pathway** | JUPAS (if HKDSE) or Non-JUPAS (if A-level) | Non-JUPAS only |
| **Scholarships** | Broader range including government schemes | University-specific scholarships only |
| **Post-graduation** | No visa restrictions | IANG visa available (2 years) |

### Critical Points:
1. **A-level students ALWAYS apply via Non-JUPAS** — HK residency does not change this.
2. HK residents with A-levels pay **local (subsidized) tuition** if they meet the residency requirement (typically 7 years of ordinary residence).
3. Non-local students pay **4–5x higher tuition** but have access to merit-based scholarships.
4. The JUPAS system is ONLY for HKDSE holders — it is not available to A-level students regardless of residency status.
